# Script Final: The Exploding Whale (1970)

**Format**: 9-Shot Vertical Short (9:16 @ 30 fps)  
**Target Duration**: ~41 - 43 seconds  
**Word Count**: 150 words  
**Pacing Target**: 3.70 - 3.75 words per second  
**Visual Style**: 2D cutout character stick figures with #24231f ink outlines in a stylized 3D low-poly environment  

---

## Shot List & Dialogue

### Shot 1
* **Shot Number**: 1
* **Dialogue**: A highway engineer packed a dead whale with half a ton of dynamite.
* **Visual**: Low-angle slow push-in on an orange-vested stick figure sliding red dynamite sticks into the sand beneath a gargantuan, 3D-shaded beached whale.

### Shot 2
* **Shot Number**: 2
* **Dialogue**: You see, in 1970, an eight-ton whale washed up rotting on an Oregon beach.
* **Visual**: Wide camera crane shot across a desolate, foggy coastline where a crowd of stick figures holds their noses around the massive grey carcass.

### Shot 3
* **Shot Number**: 3
* **Dialogue**: Nobody knew how to move it, so George figures if you blow it to bits, the seagulls will just eat whatever's left.
* **Visual**: George proudly points at a whiteboard with a cartoon explosion drawing, while two blank-eyed seagulls stand on a sand dune staring at him.

### Shot 4
* **Shot Number**: 4
* **Dialogue**: A military veteran in town begs him to stop, saying twenty cases of dynamite is completely insane.
* **Visual**: A frantic stick figure wearing an army field jacket frantically waves his arms in front of an absurdly tall stack of red wooden explosive crates.

### Shot 5
* **Shot Number**: 5
* **Dialogue**: George laughs, buries the dynamite anyway, and walks a quarter-mile down the road to push the plunger.
* **Visual**: Eye-level tracking shot of George spooling out a black wire across sand dunes, crouching behind a highway barrier, and grinning as his hands grip the wooden plunger.

### Shot 6
* **Shot Number**: 6
* **Dialogue**: The explosion shakes the ground and launches a massive cloud of sand and blubber into the sky.
* **Visual**: Sudden screen shake and flash frame as a towering brown-and-crimson volumetric smoke plume erupts from the beach into the upper clouds.

### Shot 7
* **Shot Number**: 7
* **Dialogue**: For three seconds, it's completely silent. Then, chunks of rotting blubber start raining from the clouds.
* **Visual**: George and the spectators look up with giant disc-eyes in frozen shock as heavy dark blobs tumble out of the sky, sending stick figures running in panic.

### Shot 8
* **Shot Number**: 8
* **Dialogue**: A slab the size of a coffee table drops straight down and flattens a brand-new car.
* **Visual**: A massive wet blubber slab drops with a violent screen-shake, caving in the roof and blowing out the windows of a pristine cream-colored sedan in the beach lot.

### Shot 9
* **Shot Number**: 9
* **Dialogue**: It belonged to the veteran, who'd just bought it on a sale called a whale of a deal.
* **Visual**: The army-jacket veteran stands in the foreground staring blankly at his flattened car holding his car keys, while a single terrified seagull flaps away into the horizon.
