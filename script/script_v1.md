## Shot-by-Shot Screenplay

### Shot 1
* **Duration**: 0:00.0 – 0:03.5 (3.5s)
* **Word Count**: 13 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"A highway engineer packed a dead whale with half a ton of dynamite."*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Deadpan, matter-of-fact delivery. Zero hesitation.
* **Visual**: Low-angle slow push-in on an orange-vested stick figure sliding red dynamite sticks into the sand beneath a gargantuan, 3D-shaded beached whale.

### Shot 2
* **Duration**: 0:03.5 – 0:07.8 (4.3s)
* **Word Count**: 14 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"You see, in 1970, an eight-ton whale washed up rotting on an Oregon beach. [revolted shudder]"*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: *[gag / shudder]* Disgust creeping into the tone on *"rotting"*.
* **Visual**: Wide camera crane shot across a desolate, foggy coastline where a crowd of stick figures holds their noses around the massive grey carcass.

---

### Shot 3
* **Duration**: 0:07.8 – 0:13.8 (6.0s)
* **Word Count**: 22 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"Nobody knew how to move it, so George figures—[proud chuckle]—if you blow it to bits, the seagulls will just eat whatever's left."*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Smug, misplaced confidence; a light arrogant chuckle mid-sentence.
* **Visual**: George proudly points at a whiteboard with a cartoon explosion drawing, while two blank-eyed seagulls stand on a sand dune staring at him.

---

### Shot 4
* **Duration**: 0:13.8 – 0:18.5 (4.7s)
* **Word Count**: 17 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"A military veteran in town begs him to stop, saying twenty cases of dynamite is completely insane! [desperate gasp]"*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: High urgency, cracked voice on *"insane!"*, trailing into a sharp worried breath.
* **Visual**: A frantic stick figure wearing an army field jacket frantically waves his arms in front of an absurdly tall stack of red wooden explosive crates.

---

### Shot 5
* **Duration**: 0:18.5 – 0:23.5 (5.0s)
* **Word Count**: 17 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"[dismissive scoff] George laughs, buries the dynamite anyway, and walks a quarter-mile down the road to push the plunger."*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Arrogant snort/scoff at the start; building anticipation into a whisper on *"push the plunger"*.
* **Visual**: Eye-level tracking shot of George spooling out a black wire across sand dunes, crouching behind a highway barrier, and grinning as his hands grip the wooden plunger.

---

### Shot 6
* **Duration**: 0:23.5 – 0:28.2 (4.7s)
* **Word Count**: 17 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"The explosion shakes the ground and launches a massive cloud of sand and blubber into the sky."*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Booming, kinetic, adrenaline-pumped vocal delivery.
* **Visual**: Sudden screen shake and flash frame as a towering brown-and-crimson volumetric smoke plume erupts from the beach into the upper clouds.

---

### Shot 7
* **Duration**: 0:28.2 – 0:33.2 (5.0s)
* **Word Count**: 16 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"For three seconds, it's completely silent. [sharp gasp] Then, chunks of rotting blubber start raining from the clouds!"*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Tense whisper during the silence; sudden horrified gasp before the chaotic reveal.
* **Visual**: George and the spectators look up with giant disc-eyes in frozen shock as heavy dark blobs tumble out of the sky, sending stick figures running in panic.

---

### Shot 8
* **Duration**: 0:33.2 – 0:37.7 (4.5s)
* **Word Count**: 16 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"A slab the size of a coffee table drops straight down—[wincing grimace]—and flattens a brand-new car."*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Wincing, visceral impact sound (*"oof/crunch"* nuance in the throat).
* **Visual**: A massive wet blubber slab drops with a violent screen-shake, caving in the roof and blowing out the windows of a pristine cream-colored sedan in the beach lot.

---

### Shot 9
* **Duration**: 0:37.7 – 0:42.5 (4.8s)
* **Word Count**: 18 words
* **Voiceover**:  
  > *"[heavy defeated sigh] It belonged to the veteran... who'd just bought it on a sale called a whale of a deal. [dry bitter chuckle]"*
* **Vocal & Emotional Cue**: Deep exhausted sigh, followed by dry comic disbelief and a soft sarcastic chuckle on the punchline.
* **Visual**: The army-jacket veteran stands in the foreground staring blankly at his flattened car holding his car keys, while a single terrified seagull flaps away into the horizon.
